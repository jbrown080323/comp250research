However, because of this rapid development, this has lead to oversights of varying sizes, which has lead to data breaches from various companies that do work involving data of some kind, among them being social media corporations. 
Along with the issue of data breaches, corporations also have been known to freely sell data from users, many on whether if they can be trusted with the data of millions of users and how secure it would be if they were managing the data, mainly due to hackers and now, the rise of artificial intelligence and the worries of what data the A.I models hold only add more questions to the already divisive issue. 
On the other hand, the lack of action would only let the issue of online safety grow worse overtime, so some believe that the laws should be strict for the protection of everyone on the internet, especially for the most vulnerable.

What this paper aims to do is understand how safe users of the Internet are with in the 21st century, what should be done to protect users, and whether, if at all there should be stricter laws placed.
