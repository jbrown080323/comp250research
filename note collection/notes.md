## How Facebook Uses Userdata 

"So Facebook uses our data in many aspects to suggest us using advertisement’s and in friends suggestion using our location and education area, it analyses our emotions by our timeline, status, text messages etc."(Awan et al, 2020)

Facebook, among other giants in it's position like Google, uses data as a way to understand their users. However, along with the possible violation of privacy that a user may feel from this, this also means that, depending how strong Facebook's security is, hackers can gain access to that data, which, as stated in the article, Facebook has fallen victim to before, and users are aware of this fact, to a near cynical degree.
