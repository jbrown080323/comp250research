## The Problem with Data Collection

"The absence of explicit consent for data collection is another significant issue. Users frequently lack a thorough understanding of the types of data being gathered, how they are being used, and who can access them. Because privacy policies are usually ambiguous, users are left in the dark about the scope and intent of data sharing.. Social media companies sometimes make vague statements about data usage in their privacy policies, leaving users uncertain about the extent of the information being shared. "

The ambiguoity surrounding a privacy policy is intentional, through it, it means that users would have to trust the company's web security on how it can defend its data, while remaining ignorant of how their data is being used, how much information in said data is being kept private and most importantly, whether it is safe.
