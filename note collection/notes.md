# Note # 2

### The Problem with Data Collection

"The absence of explicit consent for data collection is another significant issue. Users frequently lack a thorough understanding of the types of data being gathered, how they are being used, and who can access them. Because privacy policies are usually ambiguous, users are left in the dark about the scope and intent of data sharing.. Social media companies sometimes make vague statements about data usage in their privacy policies, leaving users uncertain about the extent of the information being shared. "

The ambiguoity surrounding a privacy policy is intentional, through it, it means that users would have to trust the company's web security on how it can defend its data, while remaining ignorant of how their data is being used, how much information in said data is being kept private and most importantly, whether it is safe.

### How AI intergration Plays a Role 

"Popular platforms like Facebook, YouTube, and Instagram hosts recommendation services to endorse content based on individual preferences backed by deep learning models. These algorithms, employ attributes like user interactions, viewing habits, and engagement to forecast user preferences to maximize retention. However, this process does come with its share of issues related to gathering, storage, and processing of personal data that may lead to abuse and pose a risk to users."(Chopra et al, 2025)

The involvment of DL models in structuring content based on a user's preferences(I.E what kind of content they engage in on the app) is worrying when potential threats to a website's security is taken account, as well as how careful a user is in regard to *what* content they interact with, due to what it may reveal to others about their own life. This becomes more of a problem when one considers how secure the A.I model and the information it processes, as well as who has access to the data, how well it is organized, and most importantly, how restrained it is when taking a prompt from a user(What prompts it can and can not take). All of this create an understanding of how the A.I is intergrated into the website.
