# Overview

# Note # 1

### How Facebook Uses Userdata 

"So Facebook uses our data in many aspects to suggest us using advertisement’s and in friends suggestion using our location and education area, it analyses our emotions by our timeline, status, text messages etc."(Awan et al, 2020)

Facebook, among other giants in it's position like Google, uses data as a way to understand their users. However, along with the possible violation of privacy that a user may feel from this, this also means that, depending how strong Facebook's security is, hackers can gain access to that data, which, as stated in the article, Facebook has fallen victim to before, and users are aware of this fact, to a near cynical degree.

Keywords: Facebook, userdata, breach
# Note # 2

### The Problem with Data Collection

"The absence of explicit consent for data collection is another significant issue. Users frequently lack a thorough understanding of the types of data being gathered, how they are being used, and who can access them. Because privacy policies are usually ambiguous, users are left in the dark about the scope and intent of data sharing.. Social media companies sometimes make vague statements about data usage in their privacy policies, leaving users uncertain about the extent of the information being shared. "(G et al, 2025)

The ambiguity surrounding a privacy policy is intentional, through it, it means that users would have to trust the company's web security on how it can defend its data, while remaining ignorant of how their data is being used, how much information in said data is being kept private and most importantly, whether it is safe. 

Keywords: Data, privacy, security

# Note # 3

### How AI intergration Plays a Role 

"Popular platforms like Facebook, YouTube, and Instagram hosts recommendation services to endorse content based on individual preferences backed by deep learning models. These algorithms, employ attributes like user interactions, viewing habits, and engagement to forecast user preferences to maximize retention. However, this process does come with its share of issues related to gathering, storage, and processing of personal data that may lead to abuse and pose a risk to users."(Chopra et al, 2025)

The involvment of DL models in structuring content based on a user's preferences(I.E what kind of content they engage in on the app) is worrying when potential threats to a website's security is taken account, as well as how careful a user is in regard to *what* content they interact with, due to what it may reveal to others about their own life. This becomes more of a problem when one considers how secure the A.I model and the information it processes, as well as who has access to the data, how well it is organized, and most importantly, how restrained it is when taking a prompt from a user(What prompts it can and can not take). All of this create an understanding of how the A.I is intergrated into the website.


Keywords: A.I, security, information
# Note # 4

### How Users Feel about their Data

" Furthermore, we cannot neglect the fact that we have a significant number of customers who are reluctant to share data with web sites. Many users who have the opportunity to use e-commerce do not use it, because they simply do not believe that a specific web site will ensure and protect their privacy."(Mekovec and Vrček, 2011)


While the internet as whole is much more secure now, at the same time, this is mainly a result of having to learn and adapt to the various problems that have surfaced as a result of various issues, whether they are hackers or general security oversights. As a result, many people tend to distrust a website's security, and try to give as little information related to time as is necessarily possible.

Keywords: Hackers, user, website

## Note # 6

### Other Measures for Safety

"One needs to settle on cognizant choices to utilize a pen name, a collaboration or to lie about one's very own information. Besides, one needs to understand that utilizing these techniques is a choice and that utilizing this alternative may serve security insurance."(Talwar et al, 2022)

Along with end-to-end encryption, even the basic measures like merely omitting important information on the internet, similar to social engineering for hackers attempting to access data in an account, can help provide an extra-level of protection along with other security measures that can defend a user's data

Keywords: encryption, security, protection
# Note # 7

### WhatsApp ata Sharing

"This year, WhatsApp introduced a new privacy policy that was essentially mandatory for the users to accept. This policy no longer gives the users the choice of opting out of sharing information with the parent company, Facebook."(Talwar et al, 2022)


By implementing this policy, this calls into question whether if WhatsApp's security is trustworthy, as while it is encrypted, this new privacy policy removing user consent in sharing information with Facebook creates an entire problem where Facebook is implying that at the very least, they don't care about a user's privacy. Coupled with Facebook being a victim to several breaches, this is very reckless coming from Facebook.

Keywords: Facebook, WhatsApp, user

# Note # 8

### Government Trust and Data
"Internet users believe that government institutions and big companies will help them to protect their information privacy. On the other hand, Internet users also think that the same government institutions will violate their privacy by disclosing personal information without their permission."(Mekovec and Vrček, 2011)

Ever since the early 2000s, goverment surveillance has been an issue, and advancements in technology has steadly increased those worries, especially with social media sites. Despite a site's privacy policy, it's still no guarantee someone is completly private, given that some criminals have been arrested because of what they have posted to social media, this is still concerning given the amount of data belonging to users across the Internet.

Keywords: Social media, data, government

### The Dangers of A.I's Learning Ability on Social Media Algorithms

"Lack of user awareness and enforceability of data protection laws across jurisdiction are the main loopholes leading to data privacy concerns. Moreover, as the abilities of AI algorithms continues to improve, intended privacy breach and misuse concerns are natural. "(Chopra et al, 2025)
